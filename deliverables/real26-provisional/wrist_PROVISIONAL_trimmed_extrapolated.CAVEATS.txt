CAVEAT 0 - THE BACKGROUND IS NOT A RENDER OF THE SCENE
  The splat cannot draw the scene from any viewpoint, including the ones it
  trained on. Rendered from registered scan camera poses, straight from the
  reconstruction, with no retarget and no wrist camera in the path, it scores
  17.42 dB median against the photographs, worst view 15.25 dB. A splat that
  holds the scene scores about 35 dB. The images show smears with no mat
  pattern and no marker.

  So the flat discs in the stills are not caused by extrapolation. Caveats 2
  and 3 below describe real defects, and they are still real, but neither one
  explains what this file looks like. The splat does.

  Cause: 10455 Gaussians over 298 views, 35 per view. Four settings compound.
    - init_points was a ceiling, not a target. The config asked for 60000 and
      the splat started with 8810, the whole sparse cloud.
    - iterations 3000, against the 30000 the method expects.
    - densify_until_iter 2000, so densification ran 17 rounds.
    - resolution 540, so the splat never saw the mat pattern at all.

  A gate now catches this. See tools/check_splat.py and src/wristview/splatqc.py.

This file is a diagnostic. It is not training data. It is not evidence for any
claim. Do not show it without these caveats.
