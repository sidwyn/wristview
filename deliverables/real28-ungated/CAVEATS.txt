What changed against deliverables/real27-ungated
------------------------------------------------
Only the scan. Same 29 clips, same corrected mount (62.1 degrees horizontal to
match the recorded wrist camera, mount pitched 12.02 degrees down), same
spherical harmonics degree 0.

  scan floor       15.22 cm  ->  7.11 cm
  views under 15cm  0 of 586 ->  313 of 1051
  splat            7,062,083 Gaussians, 26.19 dB
                -> 6,731,793 Gaussians, 25.86 dB over 1051 views
  sha256 943198be4379bbaf0901ea50becf99b9bdcd1efc8dedd31c6fdfaa4e5ed5c61a

Result: median clip alpha 0.939 -> 0.971, worst clip 0.687 -> 0.920. The clips
that were worst improved most, which is the signature of a lifted floor rather
than a global brightening.

The mid-band, which is what this was for
----------------------------------------
Alpha by decile of clip progress, old splat against new:

  decile        old     new
  20 to 30%   0.934   0.968
  30 to 40%   0.754   0.918
  40 to 50%   0.727   0.905

Deciles 3 to 5 are the reach and the grasp, when the camera is lowest. They
rose 0.805 to 0.930, nearly three times the lift of the band that was already
clean. That was predicted in writing before the numbers landed.

The other half of that prediction was WRONG: the floor was predicted near
3.5 cm and reached 7.11 cm. Both halves are recorded unedited in
runs/real27/REPORT-real27.md.

Why it is still not a dataset
-----------------------------
render.allow_extrapolation was TRUE on purpose. These pictures exist so the
coverage threshold can be fitted by looking rather than by argument. The
previous limit of 0.50 was fitted against real26bm, which was itself rendered
with the bypass on.

Still enforced: wrist cameras below the desk plane dropped, 9 frames; control
frames resting on an unmeasured hand pose dropped, 1,875; 2,645 cameras remain.

This is the BARE SPLAT LAYER. No gripper, no object, no fisheye. Stage 5 owns
those three and has never run on real27. The August deliverables in
deliverables/wrist-gsplat had all of them, so this is still not a like for like
comparison with them.
