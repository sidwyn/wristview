CAVEAT 1 - EXTRAPOLATED VIEWPOINTS, AND THIS IS THE WEAKEST THIS HAS BEEN
  The median rendered frame sits 15.05 cm from the nearest view that ever saw
  the scene, against a 15.00 cm limit. It fails by half a millimetre.
  p90 24.44 cm.
  0 PER CENT of frames sit below the scan floor, which is 8.47 cm.
  real26/a was 47 per cent below its 18.18 cm floor. That problem is gone.
  render.allow_extrapolation was set for this run only, recorded in
  run_record.jsonl and in 05_render/meta.json. It is not in the config.

  The cause is not scan depth. The scan covered 8 to 23 cm and 30 to 57 cm and
  skipped 20 to 30 cm, which is where the wrist camera sits at the 0.25 m UMI
  standoff. Covering the extremes and missing the middle is what this gate is
  reporting. A 20 s mid-height pass closes it honestly. See CAPTURE-SOP.md,
  "SCAN FROM WHERE YOU WILL RENDER FROM".

KNOWN GAP - THE OBJECT IS ONE FLAT COLOUR
  The object is a 70.0 x 70.0 x 70.0 mm box filled with a single colour: the
  median of its own mask pixels, (0.443, 0.506, 0.235), from 98,569 pixels.
  The real cube has six distinct bright faces.
  This is a domain difference between arm B, which trains on these renders,
  and arm C, which trains on real wrist footage. Any B-versus-C comparison
  carries it and it must be ruled out before any result that could depend on
  object appearance is attributed to something else. It cannot be ruled out by
  looking at the trajectories, because the trajectories are the same.
  A Sam3D reconstruction closes it. See KNOWN-GAPS.md.

TWO CUBES ARE VISIBLE, AND THIS IS EXPECTED
  The splat contains the cube at the position it held during the scan, frozen.
  Stage 5 draws the tracked cube on top, at its moving pose. Both appear.
  Where the tracked cube has moved away from its scan position, the render
  shows the drawn box in one place and the splat's ghost in another.
  The object travels 36.1 cm across this clip, so the two are far apart for
  most of it. Nothing is wrong; a splat is a static reconstruction and cannot
  remove an object that moved after it was built.
  Removing the ghost needs the object masked out of the scan frames before
  training. Not done, and not currently planned.
