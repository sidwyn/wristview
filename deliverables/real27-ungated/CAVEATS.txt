real27 wrist renders, 28 August. UNGATED. DO NOT TRAIN ON THIS.

What this is
------------
29 clips of real27 (demo_15 excluded), rendered from the real27full splat
(7,062,083 Gaussians, 26.19 dB on its own 586 training views, sha256
6fefc37950a5271d26f91812cb45b7090088393f55270176e8444a390c4013bb).

Mount: FOV 62.1 degrees horizontal, matching the wrist camera the experiment
records, with the mount pitched 12.02 degrees down so the finger tips land at
77 per cent of frame height. Spherical harmonics degree 0.

Why it is not a dataset
-----------------------
render.allow_extrapolation was set TRUE ON PURPOSE. Every clip here renders
from viewpoints the scan never covered, and 22 of the 30 real27 clips fail the
viewpoint gate. These pictures exist to fit the coverage threshold, because the
previous threshold of 0.50 was itself fitted against real26bm, which was also
rendered with the bypass on. Calibrating a gate against a bypassed gate is
circular, and the way out was to look at renders.

The gates go back on, hard, before anything trains.

What was still enforced
-----------------------
- wrist cameras below the desk plane: dropped, 10 frames. Geometry, not a
  threshold.
- control-rate frames resting on an unmeasured hand pose: dropped, 1,875.
- 2,645 cameras remain of 4,520.

Contents
--------
strips/            one 6-frame strip per clip, named by measured mean alpha
alpha_ladder.png   individual frames binned by their own alpha, 0.55 to 0.98.
                   The readability break is at 0.85 to 0.88.
midband.png        mid-band clips sampled 30 to 80 per cent through each take,
                   showing that usability varies WITHIN a clip more than
                   between clips.
worst_best.png     the three worst and three best clips.
coverage_measurements.json   per clip: predicted coverage, measured mean alpha,
                   fraction of pixels under alpha 0.5.

videos/
-------
29 side-by-side mp4s, ego source on the left, rendered wrist on the right,
15 fps, named by measured mean alpha so they sort worst to best. Each frame
carries its own alpha in the caption and a reminder that this is ungated.

The ego frame is matched to the rendered camera through the control-rate
timestamp, so the pairing is the one the pipeline uses, not a guess.
