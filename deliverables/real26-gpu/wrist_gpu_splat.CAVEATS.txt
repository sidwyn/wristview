real26 wrist view, GPU splat. Produced 2026-08-26.

387 frames at 640x360, control rate, fisheye. Supersedes
deliverables/real26-provisional, which was drawn from a broken splat.

THE SPLAT PASSES THE GATE
  3,912,607 Gaussians over 298 registered views, 13,130 per view.
  Rendered from registered scan camera poses, straight from the reconstruction,
  no retarget and no wrist camera in the path: 30.57 dB median over 12 views,
  worst 27.18 dB, best 33.54 dB. The gate needs 25 dB and 200 per view.
  Measured by gsplat on a CUDA box, not by the local MPS rasteriser, which
  scores this same splat at 7.21 dB and is not trusted. See MPS-RASTERISER.md.
  Trained 30,000 iterations at 1920 from an 8,810 point seed.

KNOWN GAP - THE OBJECT IS ONE FLAT COLOUR
  The object is drawn as a box at the measured size, filled with a single
  colour: the median of its own mask pixels. On this clip that is
  (0.357, 0.518, 0.447), a greenish grey, from 52,054 sampled pixels.
  The real cube has six distinct bright faces.
  This is a domain difference between arm B, which trains on these renders,
  and arm C, which trains on real wrist footage. Any B-versus-C comparison
  carries it, and it must be ruled out before any result that could depend on
  object appearance is attributed to something else.
  A Sam3D reconstruction closes it. See KNOWN-GAPS.md.

CAVEAT 1 - EXTRAPOLATED VIEWPOINTS
  This is the one defect here that no code change can fix. Only a capture can.
  The scan's lowest view sits 18.2 cm above the desk.
  Rendered frames below that floor: 47 per cent, 181 of 387.
  Wrist camera height: min 1.5 cm, median 18.3 cm, max 21.8 cm above the desk.
  Nearest scan view: median 10.6 cm, p90 37.0 cm.
  Stage 5's viewpoint gate FAILS on this and the failure is recorded in
  05_render/demo_0/status.json. A splat interpolates; below the scan floor it
  is extrapolating away from every training view at once.
  To fix it, rescan with the camera down at desk level.

  This 47 per cent is not the 31 per cent quoted for the provisional render.
  That figure was for a different population: 174 video-rate frames after the
  hand_valid filter, trimmed to frames 57 to 231. This render is all 387
  control-rate poses, untrimmed. Both numbers are correct for their own render.
